Artificial Intelligence (AI) is a branch of computer science that focuses on creating machines that can think, learn, and make decisions like humans.

Machine Learning is a subset of AI that allows systems to learn from data without being explicitly programmed.

Deep Learning is a subset of Machine Learning that uses neural networks with many layers to analyze complex data.

Python is one of the most popular programming languages used in AI and data science because of its simplicity and powerful libraries.

RAG (Retrieval Augmented Generation) is a technique where an AI model retrieves relevant documents and then generates answers based on them.
